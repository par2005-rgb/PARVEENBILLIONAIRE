About the coaching

A conversation without judgment.

Life can become confusing when you're too close to a situation. Talking to someone outside that situation can sometimes help you look at it differently.

These sessions are designed to give you a private space to explain what's happening, organise your thoughts and identify practical next steps.

Important:

This service provides personal coaching and general guidance. It is not a substitute for medical, psychological, psychiatric, legal or emergency professional services. If you are experiencing an emergency or believe you may be in danger, contact the appropriate emergency or professional support service.
FAQ

Questions you might have.

How long is a session?

Most sessions are around 5–10 minutes. If the situation genuinely needs more discussion, the conversation can continue.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. Just explain what is happening and what you want help thinking through.

Can I talk about relationship problems?

Yes. Relationships, communication, personal decisions, confidence, career direction and similar life situations can be discussed.

Is this therapy?

No. This is personal coaching and general guidance, not therapy or medical treatment.
